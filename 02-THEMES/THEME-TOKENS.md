## Accent usage
Primary CTA: brand gradient or controlled blue.
Secondary actions: neutral surface + subtle brand border.
Success: green, not brand magenta.
Warning: amber.
Error: red/magenta-red.

## Dark-mode rule
Do not simply invert the light theme. Tune surfaces, borders, shadows and text independently.
