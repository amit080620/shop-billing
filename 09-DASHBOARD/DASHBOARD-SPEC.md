# DASHBOARD DIRECTION

The dashboard should feel calm and information-dense, not neon.

Top:
- greeting / business context
- date
- primary New Bill CTA

KPI row:
Sales • Bills • Customers • Stock alerts

Main:
- sales trend chart
- recent bills/orders
- low stock
- quick actions

Event/reminder:
Use a compact announcement/reminder card. Avoid giant saturated blue containers.

3D:
Use 3D only for feature identity and important actions.
Charts remain clean and restrained.
